How to use:

  1. Highlight a study question or problem
  2. Press Cmd+Shift+P (Mac) or Ctrl+Shift+P (Windows/Linux)
  3. Click the assistant button to get learning hints

What you'll get:

  • Hints and explanations
  • Conceptual guidance
  • Learning-focused support

Note: This tool is designed for learning. Use responsibly and in accordance with your institution's academic policies.
